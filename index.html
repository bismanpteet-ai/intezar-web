Is Intezar only for people in India?

It’s Indian at its core, but anyone who likes the energy is welcome, wherever you’re from.

Do I need to be a gamer?

No. Games are one corner of the server. Plenty of people are here only for the chat and the calls.

Is it actually active?

Yes. There’s nearly always someone around, especially in the evenings and late at night.

What are the rules?

Be respectful, keep the bakchodi fun and not mean, no spam. Full rules are pinned inside the server.

I’m shy. Is that okay?

Completely. Lurk as long as you like — most people drift into a conversation within a day or two.
